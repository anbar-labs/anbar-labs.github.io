Privacy Policy

Wildpack (Android app) · Effective date: October 28, 2026

In short: Wildpack does not ask you to create an account and does not collect your name, email or other personal details. Your game progress stays on your device. The app shows ads provided by Google AdMob, which may collect certain device information as described below.

1. Who we are

Wildpack (“the app”) is a digital card collecting app for Android, published by Anbar (“we”, “us”).

2. Information we do not collect

We do not require registration or login, and we do not ask for or collect your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, photos or files.

3. Information stored on your device

To let you continue where you left off, the app saves the following on your device only:

  • the cards you have collected and how many copies you own;
  • the number of free packs you have left and the time when they refill.

This data is not sent to us or to any server. You can remove it at any time by clearing the app’s storage in your Android settings or by uninstalling the app.

4. Internet connection

The app checks whether your device is connected to a network, because opening a pack and watching ads require an internet connection. This check only reads the connection status. No personal data is sent to us.

5. Sharing of information

We do not sell, rent or share personal information with third parties, except for the data handled by Google AdMob.

6. Data retention and deletion

Because your game data exists only on your device, we do not hold any of it. Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage permanently removes it. Data handled by Google AdMob is governed by Google’s own policies.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time.
