2. Information we do not collect

We do not require registration or login, and we do not ask for or collect your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, photos or files.

3. Information stored on your device

To let you continue where you left off, the app saves the following on your device only:

  • the cards you have collected and how many copies you own;

This data is not sent to us or to any server. You can remove it at any time by clearing the app’s storage in your Android settings or by uninstalling the app.

The app checks whether your device is connected to a network, because opening a pack and watching ads require an internet connection. This check only reads the connection status. No personal data is sent to us.

Google uses this data to serve ads, measure their performance, and prevent fraud and abuse. We do not receive your personal data from Google. For more information, see How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and AdMob data disclosure.

6. Sharing of information

We do not sell, rent or share personal information with third parties, except for the data handled by Google AdMob as described above.

7. Children’s privacy

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, please contact us and we will help resolve it.
